## 中文说明

### 项目简介

本仓库收录了我在 2024 年和 2025 年于西交利物浦大学（XJTLU）MTH013 课程中使用的微积分课件。

课件内容主要涵盖 Dale Varberg 等人所著 *Calculus: Early Transcendentals* 的第 1–8 章，可供学生复习、教师备课以及微积分学习参考。

## English

### About this repository

This repository contains the calculus course materials that I used for MTH013 at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) in 2024 and 2025.

The materials mainly cover Chapters 1–8 of *Calculus: Early Transcendentals* by Dale Varberg and co-authors. They may be useful for student revision, teaching preparation, and general calculus study.

### Important notice

Beginning in **September 2026, the contact hours and syllabus for Calculus I were revised**. The materials in this repository reflect the structure of MTH013 in 2024 and 2025 and may differ from the course offered in 2026 and later years in terms of content, teaching sequence, contact hours, and assessment requirements.

Students taking the current version of Calculus I should follow the latest module specification, teaching schedule, and instructions provided by their lecturers. These materials should not be regarded as the official syllabus or as a complete replacement for the current course materials.
